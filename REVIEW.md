# REVIEW: Apple rejected over two million app submissions in 2025 — three checks before you submit

These numbers are from **Apple's own 2025 App Store transparency report** and from Apple's 20 May 2026 newsroom post, plus three lines in the App Store Review Guidelines. Not legal advice. If Apple updates a page, that page wins.

- [App Store Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)

## What Apple published

From the 2025 transparency report:

- **App submissions reviewed: 9,100,620**
- **App submissions rejected: 2,093,244**

That is **submissions**, not "apps." It is an annual figure, not "5,000 a day" (Apple does not publish a daily number).

2,093,244 of 9,100,620 is **23.0%** (our calculation). In the video we say "nearly one in four." That is not a phrase Apple used, and it is not 25%.

The 20 May 2026 newsroom post says: «As powerful AI development tools drive a surge in app submissions.» So if AI built your app, you're in that line. Apple does not say it rejects apps for being made with AI, and it does not publish a percentage of AI-made apps.

Three more numbers from that same newsroom post:

- **Over 443,000** submissions rejected for privacy violations
- **Over 371,000** submissions that copied other apps, were found to be spam, or otherwise misled users (one combined total; don't split it by reason)
- **Over 22,000** submissions rejected for containing hidden or undocumented features

The rule I use: **AI builds. Apple judges.**

## Three checks before you submit

These are the guideline lines the video uses, not a complete review checklist.

**1. If people can create an account, they can delete it inside the app.**  
Guideline 5.1.1(v): «If your app supports account creation, you must also offer account deletion within the app.» A support email is not enough, according to that line.

**2. Give the reviewer a working demo login.**  
Guideline 2.1(a): «include demo account info (and turn on your back-end service!) if your app includes a login.» This applies when the app includes a login.

**3. If personal data goes to a third-party AI, say so and get permission first.**  
Guideline 5.1.2(i): «clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission before doing so.» That is not a ban on third-party AI.

## Quick check

- [ ] Did you count **submissions**, not "apps"?
- [ ] If the app creates accounts, can the user delete the account **inside the app**?
- [ ] If there is a login, did you include a working demo account and turn the back end on?
- [ ] If personal data goes to a third-party AI, do you disclose it and get explicit permission first?

## Notes

- "Over two million" is the 2,093,244 rejected submissions in the 2025 report.
- "Nearly one in four" is 23.0%, our calculation, not an Apple headline.
- Don't round 443,000 to "half a million."
